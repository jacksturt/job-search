Q: Why did you decide to apply to this role at ClickUp? (required)

Owning how AI quality gets measured is the part of the work I've found most valuable. Without it, every model or prompt change is a guess. This year I timed agent tiers on real requests and routed by the result, tuned vision models against a scoring harness, built a reviewed-turn pipeline that doubled as training and evaluation data, and switched LLM backends in CI to cut cost per run. ClickUp has many AI features on many models, so a strong evaluation practice pays off across the whole product.
