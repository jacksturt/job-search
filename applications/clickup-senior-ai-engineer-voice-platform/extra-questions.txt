Q: Why did you decide to apply to this role at ClickUp? (required)

Voice that turns into actions in your workspace is the voice product I most want to build. This year I self-hosted Whisper and several TTS engines, benchmarked them on latency and compatibility, and shipped production voice messaging. I also built voice-agent memory and Go tools the agent used to act on the user's apps. Joining a voice platform team inside a product where those actions are tasks, docs and calendar is a natural next step.
