Hello Pictor Labs Team,

I'm applying for the Senior Software Engineer role because I want my work to help patients. I started college as an EMT studying biomedical engineering, and my first job was in a Harvard medical lab working on early lab-on-a-chip experiments. Coding pulled me away, but I've kept taking biology and chemistry classes on my own time and money.

This role covers the range I've worked across. At Coordinator I built a Go data gateway with scoped tokens, rate limits and an audit log, a containerized GPU vision service with async job tracking, and vLLM serving on H100s. I also moved our GPU voice services to ARM64 NVIDIA hardware, which is close to your edge devices. At Ralli I built a Next.js app and NestJS services with live WebSocket updates and Datadog tracing. Earlier, at Hydraze, I built an IoT sensor network and the platform to manage those devices.

I haven't built software under FDA or SOC2 requirements, and I haven't worked with pathology slide images. I'd be learning both from your team, and most of my inference work is from the past year rather than the 3+ you ask for.

I'd be glad to talk about how I could help get Pictor's cloud services and edge devices into more labs.
