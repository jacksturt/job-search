Q: Tell us about your experience working in an async and/or remote environment. What practices or approaches have worked well for you? What challenges have you faced?

Almost all of my last five years has been remote. At Coordinator I was a contract lead working remotely with the client, and at Ralli and Lancer I led distributed teams as founder/CTO.

What has worked for me:
- Writing decisions down where the team can find them: a short note with the options, the tradeoff we picked, and what we'd measure to know if it was wrong.
- Small, tested pull requests, so review doesn't need a meeting.
- Letting CI be the gate instead of a person. At Coordinator the end-to-end suite ran on every release merge, so nobody had to be online to say a release was safe.
- Making status visible without being asked, so nobody has to chase me.

The challenges I've hit are decisions that stall because nobody owns them, and time zones. For the first, I propose a default with a date ("I'll go with X on Thursday unless someone objects"). For the second, I send my questions at the end of my day so answers are waiting in the morning.
