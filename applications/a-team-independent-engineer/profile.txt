ABOUT

From 2021 to 2024 I worked independently, mostly in crypto. I founded Lancer, a Solana freelance platform with escrow contracts (7-person team, $30K Grizzlython win). As interim CTO at Banger I built a platform that put tokenized tweets on bonding curves, which led directly to a $250K raise. I built Particles end to end (on-chain raffles of fractionalized-art NFTs), plus real-time front ends for Cypher's DEX, Tsunami Finance's price charts and Clockwork's dashboards. I started out at AWS and Applied Intuition.

I do my best work embedded with a small team: scoping the problem, picking the simplest thing that works, and shipping something people use.
