LANCER
Headline: Solana freelance marketplace with on-chain escrow and automatic PR payouts
Role: Founder / CTO
Dates: Dec 2022 – Dec 2023
Description:
Lancer was a freelance marketplace on Solana. Clients posted bounties, freelancers applied and submitted work, and payment sat in on-chain escrow until the client approved it. I designed and wrote most of the platform: the Anchor escrow program with dispute resolution and vote-to-cancel, and a Next.js/tRPC/Prisma app with quotes, checkpoints, shortlists, and in-app messaging. I added ACH payouts through Coinflow so freelancers without crypto could get paid, plus NFT completion badges, leaderboards, and referrals. I also built a GitHub app and browser extension that linked bounties to pull requests: when a linked PR closed, the escrowed payment went to the developer who wrote it.
Metrics:
- Won $30K at Solana's Grizzlython hackathon
- 264 bounty escrows funded on mainnet by 55 clients (on-chain)
- 574 referral accounts and ~3,000 mainnet transactions (on-chain)
- Led a 7-person team; wrote 825+ commits to the main codebase
Skills: TypeScript, Next.js, tRPC, Prisma, Postgres, Rust, Anchor, Solana, GCP, Sentry

RALLI
Headline: Real-time social sports betting with on-chain player prop markets
Role: Founder / CTO
Dates: Nov 2024 – Oct 2025
Description:
Ralli let friends bet on games together in real time. I started with player prop markets on an on-chain Solana order book (Manifest), priced against live projections built from play-by-play data. I then rebuilt it as a Turborepo monorepo: a Next.js web app and a NestJS server (games, matchups, lines, friends, leaderboards, notifications, referrals) on Drizzle/Postgres, with WebSockets for live updates and Datadog tracing. I wrote the Anchor programs for bet submission and line resolution, integrated Para embedded wallets so users never had to handle seed phrases, and built the ingestion pipeline for betting lines and rosters.
