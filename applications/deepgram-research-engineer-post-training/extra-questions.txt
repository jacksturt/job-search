Q: What is the most impressive thing you've personally built or automated with AI? Describe exactly what you did, how it worked, and the measurable outcome. (required)

A multi-sport LLM fine-tuning and serving pipeline for Coordinator, an AI sports-coaching product, built in 2026 as the contract lead engineer.

What I did: I curated training corpora for five sports (football, basketball, hockey, golf, endurance), ran fine-tuning on rented H100s with a two-layer setup (a shared base adapted per sport), and quantized the results (FP8, FP4 and INT4) so they could be served with vLLM behind the app. Then I closed the loop: coaches and users reviewed model turns in the product, and a pipeline I wrote turned the reviewed turns into new training data for the next round.

How I kept it honest: I didn't trust the output by eye. For the vision side of the same product (formation detection from game film with Qwen3-VL and Cosmos-Reason2) I built a scoring harness and tuned confidence thresholds and frame preprocessing against it. For the agent side, I timed a single-shot path against the full agent loop on real requests. Single-shot was 4-6x faster, so I added it as a fourth tier rather than sending everything through the loop.

I also benchmarked self-hosted Whisper and several TTS engines on latency and compatibility, swapped engines based on the numbers, and shipped production voice messaging.
