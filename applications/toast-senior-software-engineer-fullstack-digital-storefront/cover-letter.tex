Hello Toast Digital Storefront Team,

I'm applying for the Senior Software Engineer (Fullstack) role on Digital Storefront. Online ordering is a product where a guest notices every slow page and every confusing step, and owning that kind of consumer flow end to end is the work I like most.

At Ralli, a real-time social sports betting platform I founded, I rebuilt the product as a Next.js web app on NestJS and Postgres services with live WebSocket updates, then shipped the friends, leaderboards, notifications and referrals features that drove engagement. At Coordinator I built the CI that runs our end-to-end suite on every release merge, so tests gate what ships.

I work closely with design and product, turning designs into real screens and iterating once people use them. Running experiments and shipping quickly is how a startup survives, and I'd bring that pace to a product used by millions of guests a day.

To be direct about gaps: the posting lists Java and Kotlin on the backend. I learned to code on Java but haven't used it in years, and I haven't used Kotlin; my recent backends are TypeScript/Node.js, Go and Python. I'm comfortable picking up a new JVM codebase.

I'd be glad to talk about the role.
